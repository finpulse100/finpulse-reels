Foreign money just came rushing back into India 💸

FPIs net bought ₹53,739 crore of Indian equities between 2 and 9 Nov 2026 (six trading days), per depository data. That follows net outflows of ₹35,861 crore in September and ₹34,843 crore in October.

The trigger: an AI sell-off in the US, South Korea, Taiwan and Japan, which made India, seen as a non-AI trade, look more attractive. Even so, 2026 is still a net outflow of about ₹2.41 lakh crore.
