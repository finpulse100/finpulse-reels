MRF's profit fell 26.6% even as sales grew 📉

MRF's consolidated net profit for Jul–Sep 2026 (Q2 FY27) was ₹386.99 crore, down 26.6% from ₹527.43 crore a year earlier. Revenue from operations rose 17.4% to ₹8,660.19 crore.

The reason: cost of materials consumed jumped to ₹6,552.57 crore from ₹4,577.49 crore, about 43% higher.

The board declared an interim dividend of ₹3 per share (30% of the ₹10 face value). Record date is 17 Nov 2026, with payment on or after 1 Dec 2026.
