Coca-Cola's India bottler is going public 🥤

Hindustan Coca-Cola Holdings (HCCH) filed its draft prospectus with SEBI on 6 Nov. The IPO is up to 99.75 crore shares, all an offer for sale by the promoter, so HCCH gets no proceeds. The promoter's stake would fall from about 60% to about 45%.

Apr–Jun 2026: revenue ₹4,310.17 crore (+20.46%), volume +18.61%, EBITDA margin 17.20%. But FY26 revenue was down about 9%, partly because some bottling operations were moved to independent partners.

Price band and dates are not announced yet. The issue needs SEBI review first.
