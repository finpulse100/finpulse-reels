# Evening caption — FPI U-Turn — 2026-11-11

## Instagram caption

₹2.41 lakh crore sold. ₹53,739 crore bought back. 10 days. Something just changed.

Foreign portfolio investors spent all of 2026 pulling out of Indian markets at a record pace — ₹2.41 lakh crore, the highest annual outflow ever. Then November happened.

In just the first 10 trading days of November, FIIs turned net buyers and put ₹53,739 crore back into Indian equities — the highest monthly inflow since September 2024.

What shifted: the global AI trade is unwinding, crude is falling toward $91, and India's valuations look cheap against global peers. Nifty is up 10.85% from its October 29 low.
