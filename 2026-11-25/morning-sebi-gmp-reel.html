WHAT IS GMP?

The IPO signal crores rely on — SEBI says ignore it

SEBI · PR No. 75/2026 Advisory dated 23 November 2026

Grey Market Premium (GMP) = price at which IPO shares trade in unofficial channels before listing day. SEBI says it is unverified and unreliable.
